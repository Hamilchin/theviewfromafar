The Infinity Machine, Sebastian Mallaby - Review

Sebastian Mallaby does a great job going through the history of Deepmind. I have a new respect for Demis Hassabis, and the rocky landscape he navigated with respect to Musk, Altman, and co.

It is surreal reading about events that happened in 2023/2024 as if they are history. But they are! Or will be, soon enough.
